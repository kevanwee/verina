# Artifact templates

Copy instantiated records into `private/`, never into public issues or pull requests. Templates are concise working tools; record what occurred, not imagined attendance or agreement.

## Sprint Planning

```markdown
# Sprint Planning — NN
Date/time/timezone:
Participants actually involved:
State: proposed / agreed (record decision evidence)
Product Goal:
Sprint Goal (one outcome and why it matters):
Dates and timebox:
Capacity and constraints (measured versus assumptions):

| Selected item | User value | Acceptance evidence | Size by Developers | Dependencies/risks | First task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Delivery plan and integration order:
Definition of Done version:
Scope deliberately deferred and why:
Open questions / decision owner / needed by:
```

## Impediment and decision log

```markdown
| ID | Observed obstacle | Impact on Goal | Owner | Next action | Needed by | Evidence/status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Decision ID | Question and options | Accountable decision-maker | Actual decision/evidence | Date | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

A source retrieval failure is an evidence limitation; a failing authorization test is a defect; missing human judgment is a decision dependency. Name them accurately so the next action is clear.
