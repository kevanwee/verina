## Corrections and proposition limits

- Business-contact status depends on s 2's definition and purpose; contractual names are not automatically exempt. Confidentiality controls still apply.
- Redaction does not automatically make a prompt T3 or anonymous. Context and the retained mapping can identify an individual; payload sensitivity remains context-dependent, up to T1.
- DPA documented-instruction requirements are contractual safeguards. Reg 11(2)(b) was wrongly attached to that proposition; it concerns specified countries/territories.
- A customer or provider's role must be established per activity. Written contracts and instructed processing are assumptions needing evidence before production.
- Confidentiality promises and access restrictions do not prove privilege survives external disclosure. Singapore counsel must assess actual facts and terms.
- Twenty-four hours for DI notification, 72-hour follow-up, retention periods and redaction recall targets are proposals. Statutory timelines and exceptions must be checked separately.
- A certification/contract is not a universal substitute for all transfer analysis: actual recipient, scope, destination and other applicable legal requirements still need review.
